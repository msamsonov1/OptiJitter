Mathematical Framework & Dual State Observer Inversion

1 Diffraction & Irradiance Degradation

Airy Disk & Strehl

For a laser transmitter with aperture diameter $D_t$ operating at optical wavelength $\lambda$ across deep-space distance $Z$, the far-field beam divergence angle $\theta_{\text{div}}$ and peak center irradiance $I_0$ at the Earth receiver aperture are governed by:

$$I_0 = \frac{P_t \cdot \tau_t \cdot \pi D_t^2}{2 \lambda^2 Z^2} \exp\left( -2 \frac{\theta_{\text{jitter}}^2}{\theta_{\text{div}}^2} \right)$$

Where $\theta_{\text{jitter}}$ is the instantaneous pointing offset error vector. A structural acceleration spike exceeding $0.2\,\mu\text{rad}$ RMS reduces coupled photons into the ground collector below the threshold for pulse-position modulation (PPM-64) symbol demodulation.

Governing Metric: Strehl Ratio $S \approx \exp\left(-\sigma_\phi^2\right) \ge 0.88$ requirement.

2 Thermo-Optic Emission Wavelength Drift

DFB / Seed Diode

Distributed Feedback (DFB) laser master oscillators experience optical emission wavelength shifts via temperature-dependent refractive index variations ($dn/dT$) and lattice expansion in the InGaAsP quantum well:

$$\frac{d\lambda}{dT} = 2 \Lambda_{\text{grating}} \left( \frac{\partial n_{\text{eff}}}{\partial T} + n_{\text{eff}} \alpha_{\text{th}} \right) \approx 0.10\,\text{nm/}^\circ\text{C}$$

Earth ground station receivers (such as the Palomar 5-meter telescope) deploy ultra-narrowband Fabry-Pérot or volume holographic grating solar background rejection filters with a Full Width at Half Maximum ($\text{FWHM}$) of only $0.12\,\text{nm}$. A thermal drift of merely $\pm 0.8\,^\circ\text{C}$ shifts the signal completely out of the optical passband transmission window.

Passband Profile: $T(\lambda) = \left[ 1 + \left( \frac{2(\lambda - \lambda_0)}{\Delta \lambda_{\text{FWHM}}} \right)^2 \right]^{-1}$.

3 Dual Extended Kalman Filter Observer

10 kHz DSP Inversion

OptiJitter combines a 3-axis high-bandwidth piezoelectric accelerometer cluster with an internal optical wavelength wavemeter (heterodyne Mach-Zehnder discriminator). The discrete state equation decouples vibrational force from thermal diffusion:

$$\hat{\mathbf{x}}_{k|k} = \hat{\mathbf{x}}_{k|k-1} + \mathbf{K}_k \left( \begin{bmatrix} a_{\text{piezo}} \\ \lambda_{\text{hetero}} \end{bmatrix}_k - \mathbf{H} \hat{\mathbf{x}}_{k|k-1} \right)$$

The high-speed state observer isolates harmonic disturbances caused by reaction wheel unbalance (e.g., $f_{\text{wheel}} = 120\,\text{Hz}$ and structural resonance at $480\,\text{Hz}$) while simultaneously tracking slow 0.05 Hz thermal drifts, feeding optimal command trajectories directly into dual actuators.

Control Latency: $\tau_{\text{cycle}} \le 96\,\mu\text{s}$ executed on radiation-hardened FPGA.

4 Concurrent Actuation Hardware Spec

FSM + TEC Integration

The pointing stabilizer coordinates two physically independent actuation sub-assemblies inside the optical transceiver payload bench:

  • Piezo Fast-Steering Mirror (FSM): Tip/tilt range $\pm 2.5\,\text{mrad}$, resolution $< 15\,\text{nrad}$, resonance $> 12\,\text{kHz}$, capacitive strain gauge feedback.
  • Thermoelectric Cooler (TEC): Dual-stage Peltier cooler with $\pm 0.002\,^\circ\text{C}$ thermal stabilization across a $-30\,^\circ\text{C}$ to $+65\,^\circ\text{C}$ baseplate range.
